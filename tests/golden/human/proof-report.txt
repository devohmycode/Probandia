### 4.1 Argument de preuve pour Q1

**Question.** Who were the parents of Victor Hugo?

**Sources.** The birth record of 1802.

**Analyse.** An original, made by the father.

**Corrélation.** No other evidence disagrees.

**Conflits résolus.** None.

**Conclusion.** Léopold Hugo is the father.

[§ 5.1](#s-analysis-an-victor-hugo-1802-birth)

*Relu par le contradicteur, de façon indépendante.*

> **Objection 1** (`victor-hugo-1802.birth`) — A namesake was born in Nancy in 1801. Réponse : He died in 1803 (death record, act 14).
>
> **Objection 2** — The father's name is spelled Hugot. Écartée : a known variant
