## Normalised transcription

Number 31, Marchand, Henri. On 14 mars 1870, at ten in the morning, before the mayor of Bourg-Vert appeared Pierre Marchand, farmer, aged 29, who presented a male child born that day of him and of Marie Lebrun, his wife, named Henri. Witnesses: Joseph Lebrun, grandfather, and Jean Guibert, neighbour.
