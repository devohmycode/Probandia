# Before writing a negative

**A zero without a witness is not a negative.** Search the same source for a
term that must be there (the town, the department, the publisher, a common
local surname). The witness answers and the name gives zero: the negative
holds; give its source, window, extent and witness. The witness gives zero too:
the text layer is empty or the tool is broken. That is a failure, not a
negative: say which, and what would show more.

**False-negative factories.** Each returns a clean zero, with no error.
- Silent imports: an OCR sieve without its library (`rapidocr_onnxruntime`,
  `pymupdf`) gives "0 hits". **Count the files really produced** (texts,
  images, lines) before believing a zero, and give that count in your return.
- A malformed query: a list sent for a string gives a 400 shown as a network
  failure; `OR` and quotes can give zero where plain words give nine.
- The search form is not the OCR layer: a portal's engine folds accents
  ("exact" is not exact) or truncates its list. Redo a form's negative on the
  text.
- A collapsed SRU: without un-collapsing, a whole periodical counts as one hit.
- Several editions: check which edition of a paper is online before reading
  its silence.
- An empty render, "Loading…" or a challenge page: nothing was read.

**Re-count by sampling.** When a sieve declares N issues or views without the
name, open 5 to 10 of the absent ones at random yourself. One hit brings the
negative down: change the setting and redo the whole lot.

**A whole denominator.** A negative of proportion ("21 of 238") holds only if
the denominator was gone through entirely.

**Two alphabets.** A census list may hold several series, each with its own
alphabet of streets, or follow the enumerator's round: probe three views
before searching by letter. A probe is not a full reading: only the whole
series, in each of its alphabets, proves an absence.

**An absence as proof.** A negative that answers a question
(`proves: proof`) says in `expected` why the record should be there: the
person lived there then, the register is whole. Else `apply` refuses it.
